# Human Authoring Orchestration

Omega HTML remains orchestrator. Human-first authoring owns genre, audience,
through-line, rhythm, and first prose. UX Writing owns compact headings,
labels, statuses, table headings, captions, and callouts. Conservative Humanity
may remove robotic rhythm from prose only. No worker may change Truth, evidence,
structured states, claim IDs, Composition, rendering, or visual design.

Voice Builder is optional only when the Reader Brief requests brand/personal
voice and real approved evidence exists. No profile means no voice pass.
