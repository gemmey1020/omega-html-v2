# Information Architecture

Presentation follows meaning and reader need, not component availability.
The semantic vocabulary is `verdict`, `narrative`, `key_fact`, `comparison`,
`decision_matrix`, `evidence_matrix`, `risk_register`, `sequence`, `timeline`,
`checklist`, `table`, `callout`, `technical_block`, `technical_annex`, and
`source_ledger`. These are intents, not mandatory components.

Use the least visually complex structure that communicates correctly. A
repeated card grammar without a distinct semantic reason is rejected. Annexes
hold detail that supports auditability without competing with the decision.
