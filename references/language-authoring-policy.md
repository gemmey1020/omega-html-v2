# Language Authoring Policy

Arabic-first means natural Arabic syntax and no decorative English. English is
earned when precision or identity requires it: APIs, identifiers, product
names, commands, paths, versions, hashes, and established technical names.
Do not translate those protected tokens. Avoid translating English rhetorical
structure word-for-word. Mixed RTL/LTR text protects exact technical spans.
English artifacts use the same human-first pipeline without Arabic-specific
transformations.
