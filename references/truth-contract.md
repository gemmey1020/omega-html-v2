# Truth Model Contract

Truth is the factual and evidentiary layer. Each material claim has a stable
`claim_id`, meaning, typed value, epistemic state, closure state, risk, source
references, materiality, and protected exact tokens where needed.

Normalized epistemic states are `observed`, `source_backed`, `inferred`,
`unknown`, and `not_found`. Closure and risk are independent dimensions.
Downstream copy may reword a claim but may not strengthen its epistemic state.
Truth owns no layout, theme, CSS, component, headline, or final prose.
