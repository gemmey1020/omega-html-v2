# Wave 6 authoring benchmark

This is an output benchmark from the final AHS, proposal, and technical copy
blocks. It is not a review of instruction files. The pinned human-first,
UX-writing, and conservative-humanizer instructions were applied while the
three bundles were authored; Truth Lock then checked the finished copy.

## What changed in the AHS output

- The AHS now carries all 24 requirement claims plus supporting operational facts.
- Arabic prose leads the decision surface; English remains only in earned technical identifiers.
- Numbers appear where a reader needs the fact, such as the facts block or evidence matrix. They are not added to headlines as decoration.
- Status, risk, and evidence boundary are separate instead of being flattened into one label.

## V1 vs V2

| Dimension | Result | Evidence in v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Truth preservation | BETTER | 24 requirements plus 14 supporting facts, all sourced and Truth-Locked |
| Requirement coverage | BETTER | 24/24 material requirement claims authored and composed |
| Risk preservation | BETTER | varied risk values are retained per requirement and fact |
| Evidence boundary preservation | BETTER | read-only, restore, role, device, and production-closure limits remain explicit |
| First-screen clarity | BETTER | decision, boundary, and operational facts precede the requirement matrix |

No hard-blocking WORSE result was found in the truth, coverage, risk, or
evidence-boundary dimensions.
